TEP WORLDS

Climate Crisis – logo wordmark only. Variable font; use sparingly, never for UI or body text.

Tep Worlds is a collaborative worldbuilding platform where imagination becomes infrastructure. Our identity is rooted in organic systems, crafted details, and shared authorship. We build tools that feel alive—grounded, warm, and full of possibility.

The brand balances craft and clarity: deep natural greens, sunlit accents, and thoughtful typography create a system that invites play without sacrificing precision.

05

Logo Usage

Clearspace & Contrast

Minimum clearspace = height of one node dot in the mark. Keep this area free of text, graphics, or edge of frame.

Do not recolor the logo. Use the full-color mark on light backgrounds (#f2f2f0, white). Use reversed white version on Jewel #1a6f34 or Thunder #281f24.

Always maintain sufficient contrast. When in doubt, place logo on Desert Storm.

Incorrect Uses

Stretch / distort

Drop shadow / effects

Change colors

06

Voice & Tone

Imaginative

We speak like worldbuilders—curious, specific, and visual. Invite possibility. Use active verbs and concrete nouns.

Collaborative

We are co-authors, not gatekeepers. Say "we" and "you," ask questions, celebrate contributions.

Story-Driven

Every feature is a tool for narrative. Lead with the story the user can tell, not the tech underneath.
